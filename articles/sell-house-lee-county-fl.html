Sell House Fast in Lee County FL: Cape Coral & Fort Myers Options

Navigate the shifting Southwest Florida market with clear options to sell your property on your timeline, regardless of condition or hurricane damage.

To sell a house in Lee County FL fast, you can list on the traditional market, manage repairs yourself, or accept a cash offer. Cash House Buyers 4 You delivers written offers within three days and closes in an average of 29 days, without requiring storm repairs or listing fees.

Approximately 13% of the 227 properties we've purchased since 2021 had significant storm, fire, or water damage—situations traditional buyers typically avoid. For homeowners in Cape Coral, Fort Myers, and Bonita Springs, figuring out how to handle a property that needs work or one you simply no longer want to manage requires understanding the real paths available to you.

The Southwest Florida Market Reality

Lee County has faced massive housing shifts over the last few years. While the coastal areas of Sanibel, Estero, and Fort Myers Beach are actively rebuilding, many property owners are tired of managing insurance payouts, finding reliable contractors, and paying rising holding costs. Whether you are a local resident, a landlord dealing with tenant issues, or a seasonal snowbird managing a second home from out of state, carrying a property you do not want becomes a massive financial drain over time.

The traditional real estate market heavily favors turnkey homes. Retail buyers relying on standard mortgages often cannot secure funding if a home has an older roof, open permits, or remaining storm damage. Because we operate across multiple states—including Georgia, Oklahoma, and Florida—we have the established systems and funding in place to walk you through the sale on your exact timeline. You do not have to worry about a localized retail buyer backing out at the last minute due to a strict inspection.

Contractor fraud and delays are also a persistent issue for homeowners trying to fix a house before a traditional listing. We always recommend that before hiring a team to prepare your home for the MLS, you should carefully verify their active licenses directly through the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) database to ensure they are qualified to handle Lee County code requirements.

The Financial Cost of Waiting

Holding onto an unwanted property in Lee County is rarely free. Even if the home is paid off, the ongoing costs of property taxes, insurance premiums, and basic maintenance add up quickly. If the home is vacant, the risk of vandalism, squatters, or unaddressed water leaks increases significantly. City code enforcement in municipalities like Cape Coral and Fort Myers is notoriously strict. If your lawn is overgrown or your property has unpermitted structures, the city will issue fines that eventually turn into liens against the house.

Property taxes alone can be a heavy burden. If you are unsure of what your current tax liability is, you can review your current property tax assessment via the Lee County Property Appraiser online portal. When you sell directly to an investor, you immediately stop the bleeding. We take over the tax burdens and any pending municipal liens at closing, allowing you to walk away cleanly.

What Are Your Real Options?

When you are looking to step away from a Lee County property, you are not forced into a single path. Many homeowners mistakenly believe they must either hire a realtor and endure months of showings or take a lowball offer from a local flipper. That is simply not true. We always walk homeowners through five ways to sell, allowing you to choose the structure that makes the most sense for your equity position and your personal timeline.

  • A Fair Cash Offer: We purchase the home entirely in cash, taking it exactly as it sits today. This eliminates bank approvals, appraisals, and lending delays. Our fastest close on record is just 7 days, though our average is 29 days.
  • Seller Financing: If you own the home outright but want a steady stream of monthly income instead of a lump sum, you can act as the bank. We pay you directly over an agreed timeline, providing you with consistent passive income without the headaches of being a landlord.
  • Subject-To Financing: If you have very little equity but need to move on from the property immediately, we can take over the payments on your existing mortgage. This allows you to walk away cleanly without bringing cash to the closing table.
  • Novation: We partner with you to cover the upfront costs of necessary repairs, sell the home on the retail market at full value, and split the increased profits. This is ideal for homeowners who have time to wait for a traditional buyer but lack the cash to fix the home themselves.
  • Wholesale: We match your property with our trusted network of local investors who specialize in heavy rehab projects across Southwest Florida, ensuring the property goes to a buyer prepared for the scope of work.

Our founder, Jagger Babuin, was recently featured on the podcast A Canadian Investing in the U.S. (EP419) discussing our remote multi-state approach to these exact strategies. This flexibility allows us to help homeowners from Fort Myers to Pasco County without the delays of traditional real estate financing.

Navigating Insurance and Storm Damage

If your property sustained damage during recent hurricane seasons, you might be debating whether to fix it before selling. Selling a storm-damaged house is difficult on the traditional market because insurance-repair timelines rarely match a buyer's mortgage approval schedule. We buy houses in any condition, meaning you can bypass the contractor hunt entirely.

Furthermore, the insurance market in Florida is notoriously volatile. Homeowners looking to track the latest property insurance rate filings and market changes often have to monitor updates through the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation just to understand why their premiums are skyrocketing. Rather than battling rising costs, many residents choose to sell.

If you are actively fighting an insurance claim, you do not necessarily have to wait for the final check to sell the house. In many cases, we can purchase the property as-is and allow you to keep the rights to your open insurance claim. This gives you the cash for the house now, while your public adjuster continues to work on the settlement for the damages. Additionally, homeowners looking for historical flood maps and rebuilding grants can reference federal disaster assistance programs for Hurricane Ian to understand the scope of local recovery efforts.

FAQ

Do you buy homes in Lee County with active code violations?

Yes. We routinely purchase properties with active code violations, open permits, or unpermitted additions. We take over the responsibility of clearing these issues with the city after closing, so you do not have to pay the fines or hire contractors.

How long does it take to get a cash offer?

We typically deliver a written cash offer within three days of our first contact. There are no fees to receive an offer, no hidden charges, and there is absolutely no pressure to accept it.

Can I leave unwanted belongings behind?

Absolutely. You do not need to hire a dumpster or a cleaning crew. Take your personal items, family photos, and anything you want to keep. You can leave the remaining furniture, old appliances, or debris behind; we handle the entire cleanout process.

Do you require a formal inspection?

While we do a quick walkthrough to understand the condition of the home, we do not require the formal, lengthy third-party inspections that traditional bank lenders demand. We buy the property as-is, meaning we will never ask you to make repairs after our walkthrough.

What if my property has tenant issues?

If you are a tired landlord dealing with late payments or uncooperative tenants, we can purchase the property with the tenants still in place. You do not have to go through the lengthy and expensive eviction process before selling.
