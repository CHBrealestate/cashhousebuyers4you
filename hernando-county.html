Hurricane Damage and Insurance Denials

Florida’s property insurance market has become incredibly challenging for homeowners. If your property sustained severe weather damage or you recently received a non-renewal notice, selling on the open market can feel impossible. The Florida Office of Insurance Regulation strictly monitors policy standards, and homes with older roofs or water damage are often deemed uninsurable for retail buyers.

We take on the risk so you don't have to. You can easily sell a storm-damaged Brooksville FL home directly to us. We buy the property exactly as it stands—meaning you keep the insurance payout (if applicable) and leave the massive renovation project to our team.

Your Real Options: Five Ways to Sell

Most "we buy houses" companies operate with a single tool: a lowball cash offer. We approach real estate differently. We understand that your financial goals are unique, which is why we present you with five different ways to sell. We lay out the numbers for each path, allowing you to choose the structure that best fits your timeline and tax strategy.

  1. Direct Cash Sale: The most straightforward path. We make a fair cash offer, you accept, and we close in an average of 29 days. This is ideal for homeowners prioritizing speed, certainty, and a completely hands-off transaction.
  2. Seller Financing: If you own your Hernando County property free and clear, you might not want a massive lump sum that triggers a huge capital gains tax bill. Instead, we can buy the house and make monthly payments directly to you with interest. You become the bank, securing a steady, passive income stream.
  3. Subject-To the Existing Mortgage: If you have very little equity in the home but need to move immediately (such as for a job relocation or military deployment), we can purchase the property "subject to" the existing loan. We take over the monthly payments, the maintenance, and the legal responsibility, allowing you to walk away without bringing cash to the closing table.
  4. Novation Agreement: Do you have an outdated property but want to capture top retail market value? Under a novation agreement, we partner with you. We use our own capital to fully renovate the property, list it on the open market, and then split the increased profits with you at the final closing.
  5. Wholesale / Assignment: If your property has highly specific, severe structural issues (like massive sinkhole damage or black mold), we leverage our deep network of specialized local contractors and investor partners to find the exact buyer suited for that specific rehabilitation project.
